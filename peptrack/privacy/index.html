Privacy Policy

App Name: PepTrack

Overview

PepTrack is a personal compound tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data PepTrack collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

PepTrack stores the following data exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Tracking data: Compound names, schedule configurations, and selected days
  • Logs: Records of logged entries, including timestamps and notes
  • User preferences: Onboarding status, disclaimer acceptance, display settings
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active Pro subscription
We do not have access to this data. It remains on your device and is not backed up to any server operated by us.

Data Collected by Third-Party Services

PepTrack integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

  • Purpose: Processing and managing in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Data collected: Anonymous user identifier, purchase history, subscription status
  • Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Google AdMob (Advertising — Free Tier Only)

  • Purpose: Displaying advertisements to free-tier users
  • Data collected: Device advertising identifier, general location (country/region), interaction data with ads
  • Note: Ads are not shown to Pro subscribers
  • Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Services (Android)

  • Purpose: App distribution, purchase verification, crash reporting
  • Data collected: Device information, crash logs, app usage statistics
  • Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Data We Do NOT Collect

PepTrack does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identification
  • Health or medical records
  • Photographs or media files
  • Contacts or phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Browsing history
  • Any data entered into the tracker, logs, or calculator

How Your Data Is Used

  • Local data is used solely to provide app functionality and is never shared
  • Third-party data is used only for the purposes described above
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes

Data Retention

  • Local data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Subscription data is retained by RevenueCat for the duration of your subscription
  • Advertising data is managed by Google according to their retention policies

Children's Privacy

PepTrack is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
  • Request information about what data third-party services have collected
  • Cancel your subscription at any time through the Google Play Store

Security

All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's built-in security features. All communications with third-party services use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS).

Disclaimer

PepTrack is a personal reference and organizational tool. It is not a medical application and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. All compound information is community-sourced reference data. Always consult a qualified professional.

Changes to This Policy
