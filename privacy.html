Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Privacy First: We believe your data belongs to you. Our apps work offline by design, collect minimal information, and never sell your personal data.

Overview

Soft Focus Apps ("we," "our," or "us") develops mobile applications that prioritize user privacy and data protection. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our apps:

  • Michi: Menu Helper - AI-powered menu scanner for healthy eating
  • PepTrack - Research compound reference and protocol management
  • LowLux - Adaptive screen dimmer for nighttime use

Data Collection

Information We Don't Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone) unless voluntarily provided
  • Location data beyond what's necessary for app functionality
  • Browsing history or behavior tracking
  • Social media profiles or contacts
  • Biometric information

Information We May Collect

  • App Usage: Anonymous analytics to improve functionality
  • Technical Data: Device type, OS version, crash reports
  • User Content: Data you create within our apps (stored locally)
  • Support Communications: Messages you send when requesting help

How We Use Information

Any data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and improve app functionality
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal requirements

App-Specific Privacy Practices

Michi: Menu Helper

  • Camera Access: Used only for menu scanning, images processed locally
  • Spending Data: Stored locally on your device, not transmitted
  • Currency Data: Exchange rates fetched anonymously from public APIs
  • OCR Processing: Menu text analysis happens on-device when possible

PepTrack

  • Research Data: All compound and protocol data stored locally
  • Offline First: No account required, no data synchronization
  • Health Information: We do not collect or transmit health data

LowLux

  • Display Settings: Stored locally on your device
  • Usage Patterns: Not tracked or analyzed
  • System Access: Limited to display overlay permissions

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Storage: Most data remains on your device
  • Encryption: Sensitive data encrypted using industry standards
  • Minimal Transmission: Only essential data sent over secure connections
  • Regular Updates: Apps updated to address security vulnerabilities

Third-Party Services

Our apps may integrate with third-party services for specific functionality:

  • App Stores: Apple App Store and Google Play Store privacy policies apply
  • Currency APIs: Anonymous exchange rate data from public sources
  • Crash Reporting: Anonymous crash data to improve stability
  • Analytics: Minimal, anonymous usage statistics

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Delete your data (most data is already local-only)
  • Object to data processing
  • Data portability for information you've provided

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify users of significant changes through app updates or our website.
